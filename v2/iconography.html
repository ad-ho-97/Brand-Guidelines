Iconography

Icons are small, but they do a lot of work. They help people find their way, scan details at a glance and act with confidence, without adding more words to the screen. Ours are simple, friendly and consistent, so they guide the experience rather than compete with it. Use them sparingly, and only where they make something clearer.

Foundation

Every Headout icon comes from one set. We build on the Hugeicons library and set it up to our own rules: fixed sizes, a stroke weight matched to each size, and a line and a filled version of every icon. Because they share one grid and one set of proportions, any two icons sit comfortably side by side, in the product or in a campaign.

Icons in context

Icons work in two broad places. In the product, they help people navigate, decide and act. In graphics, they support a message on posters, banners and decks.

Product

In the product, an icon is a tool. It should be recognised in an instant and mean the same thing wherever it appears. Pair it with a label when the meaning isn't obvious, match its colour to the text beside it, and keep it at its set size. Stroke at rest, filled when active, mixed for value props.

Graphics

In graphics, icons play a supporting role. They add a quick visual cue to a headline or a list of benefits, while the photography and the words carry the message. Give them room, scale them up with confidence, and use mixed icons when a layout needs a little colour.
