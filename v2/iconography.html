Iconography

Icons are the quiet signposts of Headout. They help people find their way, scan details at a glance and feel confident about what they're booking, so the experience itself stays in focus. Across product and graphics they speak one simple, friendly language: clear before clever, supporting the content rather than competing with it.

Specs

Hugeicons already takes care of geometry, grid and consistency, so we don't redraw icons. What we own is how they're set up for Headout: fixed sizes, a stroke weight matched to each size, and two variants for every icon.

Icon styles

Icons in context

Icons show up in two places: the product, where they help people navigate and decide, and graphics, where they support a message. Use this section to find the right style for what you're designing.

Product

Web, app and post-purchase. Stroke by default, filled for active states, mixed for value props, multicolour for product details.

Graphics

Posters, banners and presentations. Icons support the message and are never the hero. Mixed and multicolour styles hold up best at larger sizes.
