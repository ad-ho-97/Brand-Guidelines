Iconography

Iconography

Our icon system is designed to be clear, approachable, and instantly recognizable across every touchpoint. Built on a consistent visual language, each icon balances simplicity with personality to create a cohesive experience throughout the Headout ecosystem.

Foundation First

Every icon starts with the same foundation. Shared geometry, spacing, and optical balance ensure that the system remains consistent regardless of size, platform, or application.

Consistency

Shared construction principles create familiarity across the entire library, making every icon feel connected regardless of subject.

Personality

Our icons are functional by design but expressive in character, reflecting the curiosity, movement, and excitement that define the Headout experience.

Icon Levels

Icons exist across multiple levels of visual weight and detail — from the most refined for UI use, to more expressive illustrations for marketing.

Icons in Context

Icons should never exist in isolation. Their purpose is to simplify interfaces, improve comprehension, and support the overall experience without competing for attention.
