Logo

The Headout blimp cuts through the noise — a mark travelers spot and trust in an instant. It's kinetic by design, echoing the spontaneity and movement at the heart of what we do. More than a symbol, it's our handshake with the world. Every size, every surface, every context is a chance to show up as ourselves.

Primary Logo

This is our signature — how we write our name and use our mark. Whether it's pixel-sized on a screen or blown up at an airport gate, the primary logo is how people find us, and how they know it's really us.

Colour variants

Colour is the first thing people remember about a brand — ours is Purps. When Purps can't do its job (low contrast, busy backgrounds), fall back to monochrome. Always pick the variant that stays legible against whatever's behind it.

Logo clearspace

The blimp needs room to breathe. Clearspace keeps the logo from feeling crowded by text, edges, or other elements, so it always reads clean and stays unmistakably ours. Nothing — copy, imagery, UI — should cross into this zone.

Sub-brand lockup

Headout Partners, Headout Hub, Headout Blog — every sub-brand locks up the same way. The blimp and wordmark stay untouched; the sub-brand name sits beside it in a lighter weight. One system, many voices, always recognisably Headout.

Co-brand lockup

When we share space with a partner — Mastercard, Broadway — our logo shares the frame, never the spotlight. Keep both marks equal in visual weight, separate them with a clean divider, and give each its full clearspace.

Minimum size

The logo has to stay legible, always. Never scale it below the minimum size — you'll lose detail and recognition before it even looks "off." If space is tight, fix it with layout or whitespace, not by shrinking the logo further.

Logo animation

Our animated logo shows up at key brand moments — video intros and outros, launch films, LinkedIn announcements, presentation openers — plus idle loops for anywhere it needs to hold, pause, or sit in the background.
