Motion

When banks say no, green says go. It's energetic and vibrant, just like the world we live in. And it's our primary colour, supported by a lively secondary palette that's as at home on buttons, as it is on billboards.

Principles

Delight

Playful, joyful motion that creates emotional connection through bouncy, energetic animations. The ball's enthusiastic bounce conveys happiness and engagement, making interactions feel alive and celebratory—perfect for success states, micro-interactions, and moments where you want users to feel genuine joy in their digital experience.

Seamlessness

Smooth, continuous motion that maintains flow and context throughout user journeys. The ball's effortless travel through portals represents transitions that preserve mental models and spatial relationships, ensuring users never feel lost or disoriented as they navigate between states, pages, or modal experiences.

Dynamic

Adaptive, intelligent motion that morphs and responds based on context, data, and user behavior. The ball's shape-shifting transformation demonstrates how motion can communicate system thinking—showing processing states, data changes, or contextual awareness that makes interfaces feel truly responsive and smart rather than merely animated

Easings

Functional easing

The base ease for most of our components. The ease is characterized by its slow ease in, faster peak and smooth ease out. This creates a sort of snappiness in motion that is signature to our brand. Both on product & marketing.

Expressive easing

Expressive is similar to the functional easing, with an added anticipation and overshoot to it. Expressive motion is to be used when an interaction is directly initiated by the user.

UI

Linear

The only exception to using linear is with elements that move slow on screen, like marquees, players, carousel indicator

Standard

The easing which has an accelerating curve, a peak velocity and a decelerating curve. The element starts from rest, reaches its highest pace and then gently comes back to rest. Use standard-easing whenever the element transitions to a different state on screen.

Enter

The easing has peak velocity and a decelerating curve. The element starts off at the highest pace and slows down to gentle rest. Use enter - easing when adding elements to the view, such as a modal or toaster appearing.

Exit

The easing has an accelerating curve and ends with a peak velocity. The element begins at rest and end at highest pace. Use exit - easing when removing elements from view, such as a modal or swipesheets completely from the page. If the element is being hidden such as a drop down, use the standard easing to show that it is retrievable.
