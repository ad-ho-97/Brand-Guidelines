Typography

Typography is a core part of the Headout brand system. It helps us communicate with clarity and consistency across every product, campaign, and customer touchpoint. This system gives every team a shared foundation, so every experience feels recognizably Headout.

Our typeface

Halyard is our voice in type. Halyard Display carries the big, confident moments — titles that inspire people to go somewhere. Halyard Text carries the details that move them from inspiration to action. Two styles, eight weights, one system.

Type setting

Good typesetting requires a discerning eye. Below are some universal principles to follow when setting type that helps ensure consistency and high legibility.

Type setting

Leading, tracking and measure do the quiet work. Headlines sit tight so they read as one shape; body copy gets room to breathe. Browse the examples to see the settings we rely on.

Alignment

Left-aligned, ragged-right is our default — it reads fastest and keeps layouts calm. Centre alignment is reserved for short, symmetrical moments; we never justify or right-align running text.

Halyard Display is designed to create emphasis, not support long-form reading. Use it sparingly for moments that need visual impact, while body copy should remain in Halyard Text to prioritise clarity, comfort and readability.

Colour in typography

Suggested combinations

Black type carries every tint and mid-tone; white type takes over on the deep shades. On the brightest hues — green, blue, yellow and orange — drop black to 70% opacity so it sits in the colour rather than on top of it.

Incorrect usage

Avoid altering Halyard in ways that affect clarity, consistency, or readability. Keep casing, colour, spacing, weight, line height, and effects aligned with the Headout type system.
